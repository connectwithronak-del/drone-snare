How it works

Streaks
Every 3 hits in a row raise your multiplier, up to ×5.
Bombs
Every 5 hits in a row earns a web bomb (hold up to 3). Tap the bomb button or press B, then tap the sky to net everything nearby. Lanterns slip through.
Gunners
Red-striped drones fire bolts at you. A bolt that gets through costs 10 points. Shoot it out of the air for +25.
Planes
Passenger planes cross now and then. Hit one and the run ends instantly with your score wiped.
Misses
A miss resets your streak and multiplier back to ×1.
